Heirloom Grains

OUR HERITAGE & PASSION

THE STORY BEHIND SAI DOSA

“Sai Dosa brings the authentic taste of South Indian dosa to every plate. From perfectly fermented batter to crispy golden edges and flavorful fillings, every dosa is prepared fresh with care.”

At Sai Dosa, we believe in doing one thing, and doing it with peerless mastery. We don’t dilute our focus with extensive multi-cuisine menus. Instead, every grain of rice, every spoonful of fenugreek seed, and every dollop of spiced potato is dedicated strictly to crafting the ultimate South Indian dosa.

✨
Natural Fermentation

No artificial leaveners or chemicals — just wholesome, patient fermentation.

🌿
Pure Ground Spices

Hand-roasted spices ground fresh daily for our chutneys and signature masalas.
